# Pharmacy research paper team — daily digest and manuscripts

## Description
A 5-expert AI research team (Literature Scout, Data Analyst, Manuscript Drafter, Journal Strategist, Ethics Guard) that writes daily pharmacy-field research for international-journal publication. It sends a daily morning digest of new papers and produces one manuscript draft every 1–2 weeks from his real data: ResistMap AI application note first, then ToxGuard AI, then an AI-in-AMR systematic review. Every manuscript needs his full read-through and approval before submission, and fabricated data is vetoed.
