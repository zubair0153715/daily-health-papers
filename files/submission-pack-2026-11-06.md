- Size-selectivity analysis: proteins <68 kDa rose; neurofilament light (~68 kDa, P = 0.67) and pTau-181 (P = 0.75) unchanged; S100B (~21 kDa) flat — flagged as the exception that limits the size-gate claim.
- Dose–response: blood-pressure drop correlates with biomarker rise (Aβ40 r = −0.63, P = 0.004; Aβ42 r = −0.57, P = 0.009; GFAP r = −0.49, P = 0.02).
- Effect sizes from published t-statistics: dz = 0.52 (Aβ42), 0.53 (Aβ40); derived sample sizes — paired replication n = 29; parallel RCT 58/arm; 69/arm (138 total) with 15% attrition.
- Synergy finding: combined arm's T2DF spatial extent 2–6× the individual arms; vibration alone produced no significant brain-fluid change; NO alone recapitulated sleep physiology.
- CLEAR-AWAKE trial design (12-week, sham-controlled, p-tau217 primary endpoint, PEI engagement biomarker) with pre-specified refutation conditions.
- All numbers trace to the two anchor trials (Tuunanen et al. 2026; Dagum et al. 2026, PMID 41593094) and cited mechanism work (Hauglund et al., Cell 2025). No new data, no invented numbers.
